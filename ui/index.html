    Sharpen
    Runs last, on the output pixels. Structure ≈ is an approximation of the reference (fine texture and local structure, edges kept); Local Contrast and Focus match it closely (Local Contrast: a wide unsharp mask, negative values give a soft glow; Focus: fine sharpening, negative values a lens-like blur). Adaptive Sharpening: an unsharp mask whose radius grows with the amount; Balance Sharpening moves it towards flat areas (−100: fine texture, strong edges softened) or edges (100). Order: Adaptive Sharpening, Structure, Local Contrast, Focus.
